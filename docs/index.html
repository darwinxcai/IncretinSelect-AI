Browser application · frozen model · research use

IncretinSelect-AI

Estimate GLP-1R and GCGR functional potency for compatible, aligned incretin peptide candidates using the released sequence model.

Private by design Calculations and imported files remain in this browser. There is no backend, account, analytics service, or network transmission of sequences.
Research prototype—not an affinity or drug-success predictor. Outputs are point estimates of cell-based cAMP EC50 functional potency. They do not establish binding, maximum efficacy, safety, activity in animals or people, or a candidate recommendation.
