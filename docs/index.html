Browser application · ridge regression · research use

IncretinSelect-AI

Estimate cell-based cAMP EC50 at GLP-1R and GCGR for aligned incretin-like peptide sequences.

Local processing Calculations and imported files remain in this browser. There is no backend, account, analytics service, or network transmission of sequences.
Scope: sequence-based research estimates of cell-based cAMP EC50. Outputs do not measure binding affinity, maximal assay response, safety, or in vivo activity and do not constitute candidate recommendations.
